# Game Design Document: Olympus: War of the Gods 3D

*This document is a living specification and will be updated throughout the development lifecycle.*

## 1. Vision & Core Concept

- **Game Title:** Olympus: War of the Gods 3D
- **Genre:** Large-Scale MMORPG x Real-Time Strategy (RTS) Hybrid
- **Target Audience:** Core PC & Mobile gamers who enjoy deep, strategic, large-scale multiplayer experiences.
- **Core Fantasy:** Command a god from Greek Mythology, leading an army of loyal followers in a unique Cyberpunk-themed world. Experience both the visceral, up-close action of an RPG and the grand, strategic overview of an RTS.

## 2. Core Pillars

1.  **Hybrid Combat:** Seamlessly switch between third-person character action and a top-down strategic view to command your squad.
2.  **Mythology Meets Cyberpunk:** A unique art style blending classical Greek aesthetics (marble, bronze) with futuristic elements (neon, holograms, alloys).
3.  **Massive-Scale Warfare:** Engage in epic battles, from open-world skirmishes to large-scale guild-vs-guild siege warfare and multi-phase world boss raids.
4.  **Deep Progression:** Collect and develop a roster of playable Gods and their subordinate units, each with unique skills, synergies, and upgrade paths.

## 3. Key Features (High-Level)

- **Playable Characters:** 10 unique Gods from the Greek Pantheon (Zeus, Hades, Poseidon, etc.).
- **Unit System:** 50+ subordinate units with classes, tiers, evolutions, and set bonuses.
- **Combat System:** Action-RPG controls, skill-based combat (projectiles, AoEs, buffs/debuffs), and RTS squad commands (positioning, focus fire).
- **World:** A large, seamless open world built with procedural generation and manual artistry, featuring multiple biomes and dynamic weather/time of day.
- **Progression Loop:** Explore -> Fight -> Collect/Upgrade -> Grow Faction -> Siege/Raid -> Defend -> Trade.
- **Social Systems:** Guilds (levels, skills, wars), Parties, Raids, and robust chat/communication tools.
- **Economy:** Player-driven marketplace, crafting, resource gathering, and systems to manage inflation.
- **Monetization:** Free-to-play with in-app purchases (cosmetics, convenience items, battle pass) and subscriptions. All systems will be transparent and adhere to regulations.

## 4. World & Narrative

- **Setting:** A distant future where the consciousness of the Greek Gods has been reborn into powerful cybernetic bodies. They wage war for control of a newly discovered, resource-rich planet.
- **Art Direction:** See `/docs/ArtStyleGuide.md` (to be created).
- **Major Factions:** Details TBD, but based around alliances of different gods.

## 5. Monetization & Live-Ops

- **Primary Model:** Battle Pass, Subscriptions, Cosmetics (Skins, Mounts), Convenience (Boosters).
- **Probability System:** All systems using random chance (e.g., item drops, gacha) will have their probabilities clearly disclosed and logged for compliance.
- **Live Events:** Seasonal events, new god/unit releases, and balance patches will be the primary driver of long-term engagement.
